How it is measured

Outcome accuracy is how often the highest-probability result (home, draw or away) is what happened. Draws are rarely the favourite, so even a good model misses them.

Ranked probability score and log loss reward honest probabilities, not just correct picks. A model that says 90% and loses is punished far more than one that says 55% and loses. Lower is better on both.

Where the record comes from. Predictions are stored in a log that never overwrites an existing entry, then each match is scored once it finishes. If a prediction was added after kick-off it is flagged, not hidden.
