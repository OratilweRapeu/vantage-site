How the systems met

Midfield numbers

United: Santos and Tielemans as a pair, with Mount as the 10. Brighton: Andrés, Groß and Boscagli as a three (FotMob, ESPN) or Andrés and Groß as a pair with O'Riley and Yalcouyé between the lines (WhoScored). Either way Brighton had the central overload, and Groß was Player of the Match.WEB

Brighton's defenders as playmakers

Hadjam (wide) and Vušković (centre-back) provided the final pass for goals 3 and 4. United's front players did not stop them, which is a pressing problem, not a goalkeeping one.WEB

Wide attackers left high

Rashford and Lacey were the wide players in the front three. When they were high, Dalot and Mazraoui defended wide space against Brighton's wide runners. Goals 3 and 5 both came from Brighton wide play.

Rotation

Lacey was one of eight United changes, Darlow made his United debut, and Brighton were also heavily rotated (ESPN, SI). A rotated XI has fewer rehearsed pressing triggers. This is an alternative explanation to "intensity" that the data cannot separate yet.WEB
